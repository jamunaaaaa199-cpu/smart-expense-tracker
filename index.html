Proactive Risk Prevention

4-Stage Multi-Threshold Budget Warning System

Our automated engine constantly observes your monthly expenses against your designated limit.

Stage 1 50%
Midpoint Notice

Fires when 50% of your budget limit is consumed. Alerts you that half of your safe spending pool has been utilized.

Stage 2 75%
Attention Alert

Triggered at 75% utilization. Advises immediate curtailing of discretionary spending like dining out and leisure.

Stage 3 90%
High Risk Warning

Signals severe risk. Only 10% of monthly budget remains. Restricts non-critical expenditures.

Stage 4 100%+
Critical Limit Exhausted

Budget completely depleted! Displays precise overspent figure in real-time to prevent deeper financial debt.
